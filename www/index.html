Your songs

Upload an MP3, WAV or FLAC file. The game finds the tempo and the drum hits, then builds a chart and a drum sheet you can play along to. It works best with songs that have clear drums and a steady tempo. Your files stay on this device.

Free songs

Unlock with coins

Earn coins from every song you play and every lesson you pass. Tap ▶ to hear a 10-second preview. You have 6 hearts per song. Once they're gone, missing 3 notes in a row ends the run, and you can spend coins to continue from where you failed.

Main course

Learn music

Learn to read and play drum rhythms, from your first quarter note to post-hardcore breakdowns. Every lesson shows real drum notation that scrolls as you play. Listen first, then play it yourself. Misses never end a lesson, so take your time.
